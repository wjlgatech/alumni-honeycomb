## [Unreleased]

### Added
- `make signin` (`scripts/signin_qr.py`): a 30-day sign-in QR for the private room, shown on the owner's Mac and deleted after 30 s. The owner passphrase now lives in the Mac's login keychain (futuremake.io / owner); `discovery push|pull` read it from there too, so nobody types or remembers it. iCloud-synced keychain items need a signed app (error -34018), so the phone signs in by QR. _Why:_ Paul (2026-11-27): "do it for me end to end".
- Member identity and anti-fake rules (`data/membership.yml` `identity`, `sybil`; `member.check` requires `invited_by` unless founder; `member.duplicates` flags same contact or same name+school+years). Sign-in is a one-time code to one confirmed contact, never a password. The room accepts a sign-in link `?owner=<signed 30-day session>` (from a QR shown on the owner's Mac) and strips it from the address bar. _Why:_ Paul (2026-11-27): who sets the passphrase, how a member edits and stays the same person, how to stop one bad actor making many fake members.
- Member card (`data/membership.yml`, `honeycomb/member.py`, `python -m honeycomb member check|card`, docs/MEMBERSHIP.md). Each school claim earns a level (stated · supported · confirmed) from weighted evidence: school email, degree registry (Clearinghouse / 学信网), public record, alumni association, seen document, photo, and up to 3 independent vouches. Peers alone never confirm; one circle and relatives count once. Roles have minimum levels and hours (a student CEO needs no proof; an advisor needs a supported school). Minors need a separate safeguarding screen and observable, interruptible contact. Anyone who could profit from a cell's work discloses it. Each member runs a personal loop (purpose, season goal with Good:, weekly line, review), measured by `loop_health`. The owner's card shows in the private room. _Why:_ Paul (2026-11-27): prove alumni status without relying on a diploma decades later; post availability, interests and roles; make every member's participation a closed, compounding loop.
- The private pre-launch room at futuremake.io/honeycomb/room.html: who to talk to, what to ask each person, a log-this-talk form for the phone, and the live tally (D1–D5, rules firing), zoomable glance · room · detail. The page holds no names; it reads the owner-only `/api/honeycomb-room` (agentic-portfolio) with the same owner session as /brief. `discovery push` sends the private snapshot (people, briefs, tally) from the Mac; `discovery pull` folds phone notes back into the private interview file. _Why:_ Paul (2026-11-27): the beach card must live under futuremake.io/honeycomb, everything in one place and trackable, not on a separate claude.ai page.
- Sign-up form asks for a separate, required data consent: the details are stored on a US server (futuremake.io), used only for the reminder, and deleted on request. The form also says not to enter religious beliefs or a child's details. Reason: PIPL Art. 39 requires a separate yes for data sent abroad, and Art. 28 makes religious belief and under-14 data sensitive. agentic-portfolio's `parseSignup` refuses a sign-up without `consent: true` (the server rule, not just the box).
- Pre-launch discovery for the Dec 5 meeting (`honeycomb/discovery.py`, `data/discovery.yml`, docs/DISCOVERY.md, a generated bilingual question card docs/DISCOVERY-CARD.md). `discovery plan` picks 6–8 people greedily: 2 voices per people group, 3 overlapping hubs in the first hive's circle (complex contagion), plus 2 end users and 1 skeptic to correct for hub bias. `discovery tally` counts acts, not words: the relationship ladder, committed seats deduplicated by pseudonym, the D1–D5 targets (12X = 12 committed seats), pre-committed rules R1–R5 as code, and raised breaks that nothing detects yet. Relationship comes before business (Paul, 2026-11-26), so `felt_heard` and `gave_first` are counted first.
- Links to the sibling communities on the page (Level 1 and footer): ALL-IN AI 2027, FM-SC, Stanford Eden and the FutureMake ecosystem hub, all China-reachable addresses (Paul, 2026-11-23).
- Federation mechanisms (docs/FEDERATION.md, with research). **Synchronize:** one season clock (calendar quarters, `check.season_clock`) and a heartbeat (each hive's public feed names the current or previous season; `federation-heartbeat` workflow re-pulls weekly). **Harmonize:** one feed schema `ah-feed/1` (`honeycomb publish`), signed by charter SHA-256; older ratified charters are valid but flagged `older: re-sign`. **Compound:** closed cells become learning cards (charter P8; no names, enforced by `feed.leaks`), `learn` ranks cards for a cell, `builds_on` across hives is counted (T4), and a tag seen in 3+ cards from 2+ hives becomes a draft proposal. Charter 1.0.0 → 1.1.0 via CP-001 (founding period). Second simulated hive `example-b`.

The Meet link is never in this repo; it comes back only after a sign-up is saved. On other hosts the form points to futuremake.io/honeycomb.
- The honeycomb model as data (`data/honeycomb.yml`): a cell = 1 CEO + 2–3 alumni advisors + 2–3 core + 2–3 apprentices for one 3-month season, with roles that switch between cells. It is the North Star mechanism: everyone is the CEO of something.

### Investigated / Rejected
- **Setting the futuremake.io owner passphrase to "2026" for the MVP**: rejected. The same key guards confidential briefs and every owner write on futuremake.io; a 4-digit code is 10,000 guesses. Replaced by a 30-day sign-in QR shown only on the owner's Mac (and the emailed recovery link once `RESEND_API_KEY` is set).
- **A paid tool marketplace inside the hive**: not built. It contradicts P7 (no fees, no selling). Tools are free inside; paid tools live on the maker's own site, linked with the profit disclosed. Changing P7 is a founders' decision.
- **A diploma as the single gate, or a blockchain credential**: rejected. Old graduates often can't get one (学信网 has no online record before about 2002), and a diploma says nothing about safety with minors. If the network ever issues credentials, W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0 / Open Badges 3.0 are the standard, not a token.
- **Picking the 8 most-connected people (by reach)**: rejected. Reach maximizes how many people *hear* about it. Leading or advising a 3-month project is a high-commitment act, and those spread through overlapping ties (Centola's complex contagion). So the plan reserves 3 picks in one circle and covers each people group twice.
- **Asking "would you join / is this valuable?"**: rejected. Hypothetical questions get compliments (The Mom Test). The card asks only about past events and ends with an ask whose answer is an act.
- **A central federation database of members**: rejected. Hives share learnings, not people (federated-learning principle). Only public feeds cross schools.
- **An LLM to match learning cards across hives**: rejected for now. Tag-plus-word ranking is arguable and enough at dozens of cards; measure misses first.
- **Redirecting `/honeycomb` → `/honeycomb/` on futuremake.io**: shipped briefly, rejected. Next.js strips trailing slashes, so the two redirects looped (308 ↔ 308). Replaced by serving `/honeycomb` and a self-computed `<base href>`.
- **A token or on-chain registry for the "web3 committee"**: rejected. Token issuance and trading are illegal in mainland China (2021-09-24 notice), and minors' data must not sit on a public ledger. A signed hash plus git votes verifies membership and governance for the first dozens of hives.
- **A hosted app with logins and a database for v0.1**: rejected for now. A 10–50 person hive runs on one YAML file; build it when an operator says the file is the bottleneck.
- **An LLM matcher**: rejected for v0.1. With 10–50 people, rule-based reasons a human can argue with beat a model's opaque ranking.
- **"Alumni Companion" (one-to-one companion pairs)**: the first draft this session. Replaced by the honeycomb, because pairs don't make anyone a CEO and don't switch roles.
- **Relying on `*.vercel.app` for China**: rejected by measurement (0/6 mainland probes, decoy DNS answers). A custom domain on Vercel reached 6/6, so the durable fix is a domain (D-DOMAIN), not a host change.
